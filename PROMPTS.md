## 1. Concept Formula
$$\text{Product} + \text{Emotion} + \text{Ritual} + \text{Environment} + \text{Transformation}$$

| Element | Chandu Momos Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Product** | Small-batch, hand-folded Himalayan-style momos (dumplings) |
| **Emotion** | Warmth, steam, home — being fed by someone who cares |
| **Ritual** | Folding the dough, dropping it in boiling water, sharing one plate with many hands |
| **Environment** | Warm amber studio light on a soft caramel-brown backdrop; later, a dark green editorial home |
| **Transformation** | Raw dumpling $\rightarrow$ filling exposed $\rightarrow$ into the pot $\rightarrow$ cooked $\rightarrow$ plated $\rightarrow$ shared |
